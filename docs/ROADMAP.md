## Stages

Stages follow the owner's implementation order. Each stage ends with build, Playwright screenshots, console check, DEVLOG, a playable published build and a status update here.

### Stage 1: fix the foundation
1. **Stairs and collisions** (priority). Audit every solid thing in the world against its collider: stairs of every kind, stoops and entrance steps, landings, railings, parapets, walls, benches, bins, lamps, signal poles, trees, monuments, piers, fences. Fix the character controller (grounding, step climbing, slopes, jumping, never falling through). F4 physics debug view. Automated stair tests. *Status: partial. Integrated with the real map on s1-integrate (2026-11-05): of the 85 Overture steps features built as stairs, 79 are climbed up and down by the `stairs` scenario with held keys (ends fitted to the built ground, stairs trimmed at doors and the map edge, joined where they meet, walls left out inside other stairwells); 6 are not (2 coming off a canal footbridge deck behind its railing, a switchback, a 69-degree bend, 2 in a cluster of overlapping pieces; DEVLOG). On the real map `physaudit` is not clean: 504 side-probe and 140 floor mismatches (railings, stair riser offsets up to 0.29 m on the re-planned flights, lamp and bench colliders 0.1-0.4 m long; DEVLOG), and `fallthrough` found 0 drops through the floor but 1 capsule held off the ground in 1,303 landings. On the knowledge-based map (branch s1-physics, independently reviewed and fixed): audit, colliders, stairwells, controller numbers, `physaudit` / `stairs` / `fallthrough` scenarios, F4. `stairs` and `fallthrough` pass on both maps (fallthrough also with 10,000 drops on another seed); `map.heightAt` now reads the walking-surface colliders themselves. Left over (DEVLOG): a 20 m slot to the safety floor where the canal meets Rock Creek, the metro canopy's curved glass against its collider slabs, and frontage strips up to 0.45 m below the sidewalk on steep streets.*
2. **Real 1:1 map data.** Replace the knowledge-based Georgetown with real data: Overture Maps (roads with names, classes, one-ways, bridges and levels; sidewalks, crossings, steps and paths; building footprints and heights; places by category; water and land use) and real terrain from public elevation tiles. About 2 x 2 km with a clean edge. Real street names, invented shop names. Attribution in the pause menu and README. *Status: integrated (s1-integrate, 2026-11-05): Georgetown from real data is now the default map (`?map=grid` keeps the grid city); attribution in the pause menu and README matches the data. Done for the data (`npm run import-overture`, branch s1-realdata: real roads, paths, buildings, places by category, water, parks, signals and stop signs, terrain; licences split into three files; attribution in the pause menu, README and `src/data/georgetown.LICENSE.md`). Partial for the world: the tunnels (K St underpass, Potomac Freeway portal) are not rendered, the west, north-west, east and south-east crop edges still cut the street grid, lane counts are estimates (Overture has none). Traffic, police, crowds, parked cars, taxi, theft and dealerships run on it. Independent review (verify-realdata): one-ways match Overture, Washington Circle is a closed counter-clockwise ring at its real centre, landmarks and heights where they belong, map geometry identical across seeds; fixed the Key Bridge north end (38 m -> 23.7 m, a clearance ratchet), invisible walls over concave buildings, a church tower in Q St, cars circling at one-way map exits, and a real business name among the park places. geodrive completed with no damage in 1 of 2 runs on the reviewed head (the other stuck at a 0.32 m step on K St); 0 failed yields, 1 AI red run in 316 crossings.*
3. **Roads and terrain.** Correct widths and lane counts, logical connections, crossings, signals, parking, alleys; smooth drivable road surfaces with no artificial bumps or broken seams; keep natural hills off the road. *Status: partial. Integration (s1-integrate): lanes and walk nodes now lie on the surface that is drawn where two road meshes overlap (mapcheck height mismatches 0); no bridge abutment across a street under a deck end (it stopped the gate drive on K St); the gate drive (`geodrive`) completes with no damage. Branch s1-realdata: widths measured from the mapped sidewalks, junction heights relaxed to grade limits, plateaus eased, overlapping carriageways and roads crossing other junctions' plateaus conform, deck ends meet their streets (bounded lift), lanes lie on the collider surface; road audit (`roadaudit`) and drive test (`roaddrive`) with limits in `TUNING.geo.roadLimits`. The median road is smooth and the K St / Washington Circle walls the gate drive hit are gone; the review fixed the Key Bridge / Whitehurst west end ramps, a ridge across L St and Pennsylvania Ave at a sharp merge and K St's sag onto its Rock Creek bridge (road audit grade samples over the limit 5,285 -> 2,994, lane-to-surface gaps 3,947 -> 1,875; drive test worst 32.4 -> 21.0 m/s2, 11 of 48 runs inside the limits). Still over the limits in places: service-road mouths, steep pieces between flat plateaus, the Whitehurst east end over K St, a 0.32 m step on K St westbound at 396-415 (numbers in DEVLOG). mapcheck: 0 errors, 0 height mismatches, 93.5 % one-way-connected lanes, 100 % walk connectivity every run, but its drive part (a followed AI car within 3 m of its lane for 30 choices) passes only about one run in three.*
4. **PBR look.** A material system with procedural PBR textures (asphalt, worn markings, concrete, Georgetown brick sidewalks, granite kerbs, brick in several bonds and colours, painted brick, stone, stucco, clapboard, slate and metal roofs, glass with reflections, window frames, metal, wood, grass, bark, cobbles and old tram tracks), image-based lighting from the sky, filmic tone mapping, soft shadows that cover the city, fog and haze, night lighting, Low/Medium/High presets, no repetition across buildings. *Status: partial. Independent review (verify-look) fixed: sky-mirror "frost" on backlit leaf cards (now translucent), black sky at 19:00 (sun arc now matches the day/night keys), dark-blue holes in the rain sky, a 4 m oval lattice on tar roofs, a full shader recompile on entering a parked car, per-frame program churn in the shadow pass. ### Stage 2: make the world interactive
Universal object components (static / dynamic / interactive); dynamic props that sleep and activate near the player; universal pickup and drop (E, G) for anything loose and hand-sized; inventory screen (I) with icons, categories, weight and size limits, stacking, inspect, use, containers; the held item visible in the hand; persistent object ids and transforms; save system (IndexedDB, versioned, migrations); ownership (player, NPC, business, public); reusable building entrances and procedural interiors (deterministic per building id, multi-floor, colliding stairs, furniture, shelves stocked with products). *Status: not started (current hotbar items are the seed of it).*

### Stage 3: make the city navigable
Minimap upgrade (orientation, nearby roads, destination, businesses), big map on M or minimap click (zoom, pan, names, search, click buildings and businesses, favourites, home, set and clear destination), GPS routing on the road graph (driving) and walk graph (walking) with re-routing; routes drawn only on the maps. *Status: not started (minimap and both routers exist).*

### Stage 4: make the city alive
NPC personalities, interests, jobs, homes and workplaces; daily schedules (work, home, shops, meals, sleep, meeting other NPCs) with cheap off-screen simulation; short- and long-term memory with summaries; relationship states (stranger, acquaintance, friend, close friend) that grow from varied interactions, not repetition; NPCs who recognise you and start conversations; offline ScriptedProvider upgraded to use memory; optional LLMProvider behind a server proxy only with the owner's permission. *Status: partial (chat, score, 5 memories).*

### Stage 5: daily life
Health, hunger, energy and money in a clean HUD; hunger and tiredness that nudge but never block; sleep suggestion; starting home (sleep, store items, furniture, decorate); renting or buying property; restaurants (menu, order, pay, eat animation, hunger restored; different menus, prices, interiors, hours); shops and supermarkets with physical products, checkout and receipts; business opening hours. *Status: not started.*

### Stage 6: consequences and activities
Wanted levels with clear transitions; police on foot and in cars; arrest interaction (short non-graphic sequence, also from inside a vehicle); fines scaled to severity and finances; jail as a separate scene (cells, corridors, yard, NPCs, sentence timer, simple activities, release back to the city, optional fictional puzzle escape); shoplifting and trespass as crimes; death ends the run: standard mode with checkpoints, hardcore mode; optional jobs (taxi, deliveries, restaurant work, office job with a maths test, errands, vehicle challenges, property management); short skippable contextual animations. *Status: partial (heat, police chase, witnesses, taxi, death screen).*

### Stage 7: polish
Performance (streaming, LOD, compression, pooling, activation zones), graphics and audio, environmental detail (signs, crossings, street furniture, ambient sound), expanding the city beyond 2 km with coherent procedural surroundings, persistence and integration testing. In-game phone (contacts, messages from NPC friends, maps and GPS, jobs, money, notifications). *Status: not started.*

## Decisions taken (owner can overturn)
- **Map data: Overture Maps + public elevation tiles.** OpenStreetMap and Overpass are blocked from the build container, but Overture's open data (OSM-derived roads and buildings, ODbL; places mostly CDLA-Permissive-2.0, with some Apache-2.0 Foursquare and CC0 records) and AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS 3DEP, about 3.7 m at z15) are reachable, so Georgetown becomes real geometry. georgetown.json then counts as an ODbL derivative database that we publish with notices; places stay in a separate file, and so do the few Esri CC BY 4.0 building footprints (or they are dropped). Details: docs/research/map-data.md. The knowledge-based generator stays as a fallback.
- **Textures generated in code for now.** CC0 texture sites (ambientcg.com, polyhaven) are blocked by the container's network policy. Procedural PBR maps are original by construction; CC0 photo textures can be added if the owner allows those hosts.
- **Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles: research only.** They need a Google Cloud billing account and an API key served to the browser, mandatory Google attribution, no caching or extraction, and the mesh has no separate buildings, doors or collision meaning. Not used for gameplay; at most an optional viewer later, only with the owner's explicit go-ahead and written confirmation from Google, because a page showing only Google's tiles is legally doubtful under ToS 3.2.3 (d) and (e). Full write-up: docs/research/.
- **Interiors in place.** A building's interior is generated inside its own footprint when the player approaches its door (deterministic from the building id), so doors line up with the street and no loading screen is needed; one or two interiors exist at a time.
- **Controls change.** M opens the map (mute moves to the pause menu), G drops (Q stays as an alias), I opens the inventory.
- **No paid APIs.** NPC conversations stay offline (ScriptedProvider) until the owner approves an LLM provider and a small proxy server.
